A closer look at a complete mechanical system.

Project overview

From assembly to internal detail.

The complete model brings together two cylinder banks, intake and exhaust routing, and the rotating assembly. The gallery follows the design from its exterior to front and side sections, then into an exposed view of the piston and valve-train mechanisms.

Assembly highlights

  • V-shaped cylinder-bank arrangement
  • Pistons, connecting rods, and crankshaft
  • Intake, exhaust, and valve-train packaging
